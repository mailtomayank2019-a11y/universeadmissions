Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about online admissions

Are online degrees valid in India? +
Yes! UGC approved online degrees are completely valid and equal to regular campus degrees as per NEP 2020. They are accepted by employers, government departments and for higher studies.
Is your counselling really free? +
Absolutely! Our counselling is 100% free with zero hidden charges. We earn through university partnerships — so you never pay us anything. No obligations either!
Can I study while working full time? +
Yes! Online and distance programs are specifically designed for working professionals. You can study at your own pace, on weekends or evenings — no need to leave your job!
Which universities do you work with? +
We work with 50+ UGC-DEB approved universities including NMIMS Global, Amity Online, Manipal University, LPU Online, Symbiosis, Jain University, IGNOU, Chandigarh University and many more.
How long does the admission process take? +
Typically 1-2 weeks from counselling to final admission. We handle all documentation and paperwork so the process is smooth and fast for you!
What documents are needed for admission? +
Basic documents include 10th and 12th marksheets, graduation certificate (for PG programs), ID proof and passport photo. Our team guides you through the complete documentation process!
