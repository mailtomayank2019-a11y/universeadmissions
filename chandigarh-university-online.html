About Chandigarh University Online

Chandigarh University Online is one of India's rapidly growing online education platforms offering industry-focused undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs. The university combines academic excellence with modern digital learning tools to support students and working professionals.

The university provides flexible learning, expert faculty support, placement assistance and career-oriented curriculum designed to meet industry requirements.
