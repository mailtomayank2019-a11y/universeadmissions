NMIMS Online MBA Fees 2026

NMIMS Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) is one of India's most recognized institutions offering online management education. The NMIMS Online MBA program is designed for working professionals, entrepreneurs and graduates who wish to enhance their management skills while continuing their professional commitments.

NMIMS Online MBA Fees 2026

The total fee for the NMIMS Online MBA program is approximately ₹2,10,000 (subject to university updates). Students may also have access to EMI payment options to make the program more affordable.

  • Total Program Fee: Approx. ₹2,10,000
  • EMI Options: Available
  • Payment Mode: Online
  • Fee Structure: Subject to university revision

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates should possess a bachelor's degree from a recognized university. Working professionals and fresh graduates are both eligible to apply, subject to university admission requirements.

Admission Process

  1. Complete the online application form.
  2. Upload academic and identity documents.
  3. Pay the applicable registration fee.
  4. Document verification by the university.
  5. Receive enrollment confirmation and LMS access.

Program Duration

The duration of the NMIMS Online MBA program is generally 2 years. Students can access live lectures, recorded sessions, assignments and learning resources through the online learning platform.

Specializations Offered

  • Marketing Management
  • Financial Management
  • Human Resource Management
  • Operations & Data Science
  • Business Management
  • International Trade Management
  • Retail Management
  • Banking & Finance Management

Career Opportunities After NMIMS Online MBA

Graduates can explore opportunities in marketing, finance, consulting, operations, project management, business development and leadership roles. The program is designed to strengthen strategic thinking, leadership capabilities and decision-making skills.
