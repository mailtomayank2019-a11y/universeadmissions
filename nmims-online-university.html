About NMIMS Online

NMIMS Centre for Distance and Online Education (CDOE) is among India's most recognized institutions for online and distance learning. The university offers industry-oriented programs designed for working professionals, entrepreneurs, and graduates seeking career advancement through flexible education.

NMIMS Online programs combine academic excellence with digital learning technology, enabling students to access lectures, assignments, assessments, and faculty support from anywhere in India.
