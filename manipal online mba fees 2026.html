Manipal University Online MBA Fees 2026

Manipal University is one of India's largest and most established private university groups, and its online MBA carries the same NAAC-accredited academic weight as its on-campus programs. Here's what to know before applying in 2026.

Eligibility

A recognised bachelor's degree of at least 3 years' duration with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories). No entrance exam is required for most intakes — admission is largely merit and document based.

Duration & Mode

2 years, fully online, split across 4 semesters, with coursework delivered through Manipal's online learning platform alongside recorded and live faculty sessions.

Fee Structure

Total program fees typically range from ₹1 lakh to ₹1.8 lakh depending on specialization, payable semester-wise with EMI options. Confirm the exact current fee slab with a free counselling session, as fee structures are revised periodically.

Specializations

Popular specializations include Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations, and Information Technology.
