What is an Online MBA?

An Online MBA is a postgraduate management program that allows students and working professionals to earn an MBA degree without attending regular on-campus classes. Online MBA programs provide flexibility, industry-relevant curriculum, recorded lectures, live sessions, and recognized degrees from UGC-entitled universities.

Online MBA Fees 2026

Online MBA fees vary by university. Students can expect total program fees ranging from approximately ₹80,000 to ₹2,50,000 depending on the university, specialization, scholarships and payment plans.

Why Choose an Online MBA?

  • Learn from anywhere
  • Continue your job while studying
  • Affordable compared to regular MBA programs
  • Industry-oriented curriculum
  • Access to live and recorded lectures
  • Career advancement opportunities

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an Online MBA valid?
Yes, Online MBA degrees from UGC-entitled universities are valid and recognized.

Can working professionals apply?
Yes, Online MBA programs are specifically designed for working professionals.

Can fees be paid through EMI?
Many universities provide EMI and installment payment options.
