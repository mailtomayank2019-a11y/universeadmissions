MUJ Online MBA Fees 2026

Manipal University Jaipur's online division offers UGC-DEB approved degrees with strong placement support. Here's what to know about fees and eligibility for 2026.

Eligibility

A recognised bachelor's degree with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks from a UGC-recognised university. No entrance exam required for most intakes.

Duration & Mode

2 years, fully online, across 4 semesters, with digital study material and access to recorded and live faculty sessions.

Fee Structure

Total program fees typically range from ₹1 lakh to ₹1.7 lakh depending on specialization, payable semester-wise with EMI options. Confirm the current exact fee slab through a free counselling session.

Specializations Available

Marketing, Finance, HR, and Operations are commonly available specializations.
