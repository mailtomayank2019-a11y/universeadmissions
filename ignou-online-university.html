About IGNOU Online

IGNOU is India's largest open university and a pioneer in distance and online education. The university offers affordable and flexible learning opportunities for students, working professionals and lifelong learners across India and abroad.

With a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and certificate programs, IGNOU enables learners to pursue higher education without disrupting their professional or personal commitments.
