NMIMS Online MBA Eligibility Criteria 2026

To apply for the NMIMS Online MBA program, candidates must have completed a Bachelor's degree from a recognized university. The program is designed for graduates and working professionals looking to upgrade their management skills through flexible online learning.

Educational Qualification

  • Bachelor's degree from a recognized university.
  • Minimum qualifying marks as prescribed by NMIMS.
  • Final-year students may apply subject to university guidelines.

Work Experience

Work experience is not mandatory for admission. However, professionals with industry experience often benefit from classroom discussions and practical business applications.

Documents Required

  • 10th Marksheet
  • 12th Marksheet
  • Graduation Marksheet
  • Government Photo ID
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Signature

NMIMS Online MBA Admission Process 2026

The admission process for NMIMS Online MBA is simple and completely online. Students can complete the application from anywhere in India by following these steps.

  1. Visit the official admission portal or apply through Universe Admissions.
  2. Complete the online application form.
  3. Upload educational documents and ID proof.
  4. Pay the application or registration fee.
  5. University verifies the documents.
  6. Receive admission confirmation and LMS access.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for NMIMS Online MBA?

Any graduate from a recognized university can apply for the NMIMS Online MBA program.

Is work experience mandatory?

No. Fresh graduates and working professionals are both eligible.

Is the NMIMS Online MBA degree valid?

Yes. NMIMS Online programs are UGC-DEB entitled and widely accepted.

Can I pay the fees through EMI?

Yes. EMI and installment options are available for eligible students.

How long is the course?

The Online MBA duration is 2 years.
